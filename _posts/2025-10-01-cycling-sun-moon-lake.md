Tucked into the lush highlands of Nantou County at 748 meters above sea level, **Sun Moon Lake (日月潭)** is Taiwan’s largest alpine body of water. Named for its unique geography—the eastern section resembles the sun while the western side curls like a crescent moon—it is world-renowned for its morning mist, tranquil emerald water, and internationally acclaimed cycling paths.

![Alishan and Central Taiwan Mountain Forests]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/img/forest.jpg)

## The Bikeway Experience: Shuishe to Xiangshan

While ambitious cyclists can tackle the full 30 km perimeter loop with steep elevation gains, the gentlest and most photogenic stretch is the **Xiangshan Bikeway** (approx. 6 km each way).

* **Tongxin Bridge & Yongjie Bridge**: Architectural pedestrian and cyclist bridges arching gracefully over tranquil bays.
* **Overwater Wooden Boardwalk**: Built on stilts directly over the water, giving you the sensation of cycling across the surface of the lake.
* **Xiangshan Visitor Center**: Designed by celebrated Japanese architect Norihiko Dan, this sweeping concrete pavilion blends seamlessly into the natural topography with panoramic lake views.

## Practical Traveler Tips

1. **Renting a Bike**: Numerous rental shops operate right outside **Shuishe Pier**. You can rent traditional road bikes, cruisers, or pedal-assist e-bikes (great for gentle cruising without breaking a sweat).
2. **Best Time of Day**: Start early between 7:00 AM and 9:00 AM. The lake is serene, the morning fog clings to surrounding mountain peaks, and tourist ferry wakes are minimal.
3. **Ferry Boats**: You can buy a hop-on hop-off boat pass connecting Shuishe, Xuanguang Pier (famous for Ah-Po's herbal tea eggs), and Ita Thao (the vibrant indigenous Thao village).
